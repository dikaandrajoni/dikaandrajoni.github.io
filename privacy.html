Privacy Policy: Sakato Bot

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who uses this app

Sakato Bot is a private tool used only by its owner, Dika Andra Joni, to manage the owner's own YouTube channel (Sakato AI Office). It is not offered to the public and no other people sign in to it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google OAuth, Sakato Bot uses the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API) to access:

  • the owner's channel information, videos, playlists and statistics;
  • comments on the owner's videos, in order to read and reply to them;
  • video files and details the owner chooses to upload or edit.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests (for example "show this week's views" or "upload this video as private").
  • The OAuth token is stored on the owner's private server, readable only by the bot's system account. Channel data is fetched when needed and is not kept in a separate database.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • To answer requests, text such as video titles or comments may be sent to the AI model provider that runs the assistant (for example Google Gemini), under that provider's terms.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to data held by Google.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or through the Google security settings page. After revoking, the stored token stops working immediately, and the owner deletes it from the server.

Contact

Ringkasan

Sakato Bot hanya dipakai pemiliknya untuk mengelola channel YouTube miliknya sendiri. Data tidak dijual atau dibagikan. Token login disimpan di server pribadi pemilik dan bisa dicabut kapan saja di myaccount.google.com/permissions.
