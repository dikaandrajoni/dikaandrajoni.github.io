Sakato AI Office

Saiyo sakato: many agents, one answer.

Sakato AI Office is a personal project by Dika Andra Joni: a 3D virtual office where a team of AI agents (CEO, developers, traders, analysts) writes MetaTrader 5 tools, runs backtests, tests trading ideas against real market history and reports through Telegram.

The Sakato AI Office YouTube channel documents how it is built and publishes honest test results, including the strategies that fail.

Sakato Bot

Sakato Bot is the owner's private assistant. It uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API only to help the owner manage their own channel: read channel statistics and comments, upload videos as private drafts, edit video details and organise playlists. It is not offered to other users.
